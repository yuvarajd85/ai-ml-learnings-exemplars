> **Note:** Pareto Principle: 80% of the users of any application, only use 20% of the features it offers.
> 
> **MoSCoW Principle:**
> - M - Must have:  Critical, non-negotiable requirements without which the project or product is considered a failure.
> - S — Should have: Important and high-priority items that are not strictly critical for immediate delivery, but are included if possible.
> - C — Could have: Desirable, optional features or tasks that add value but have a small impact if left out.
> - W — Won't have: Items agreed by stakeholders to be out of scope for the current timeframe or release, though they may be reconsidered later.
